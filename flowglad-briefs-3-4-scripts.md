# Flowglad Creator Briefs 3 + 4: hooks, scripts and filming plan

**Source:** the Notion page [Flowglad Creator Brief](https://app.notion.com/p/Flowglad-Creator-Brief-3f15035caf28804786f4ce475869d0e3), rows **Brief3** and **Brief4** in the Briefs table, Agencies persona. ## Shared context (from the Notion brief)
- **What Flowglad does:** it connects to an agency's Google Ads and GA4 accounts, one client at a time, and runs the jobs agencies do by hand: drafting each client's weekly report, and finding search terms that spend without converting. *Nothing changes in a client's account until someone on the team approves it.*
- **Who's watching:** owners of Google Ads agencies, from solo freelancers to teams of about 15. Most are former PPC managers who still run accounts, look after the key clients and do the selling. They judge everything by one question: *does this make my agency more money?*
- **The message is revenue, not time.** Don't sell "hours back" or "automate your reporting". Brief 3 is about keeping clients longer. Brief 4 is about growing revenue from the clients an agency already has.
- **Your role:** talk to camera like one agency person to another. Natural, a bit blunt, no hype. Use their words: accounts, clients, search terms, negatives, the weekly report, the client call.

**CTA (from the brief, one keyword per video):** `Comment [WORD] and I'll DM you the template!`

- Brief 3: **REPORT**. Brief 4: **NEGATIVES**. Each keyword says which template the viewer gets.
- The user's Google Doc used `Try Flowglad. Link below.`; the comment version was chosen to drive comments.

## Which 3 hooks to film (from `experimentation-and-ab-testing`)
The brief says *"Pick three hooks and film each one over the same body."* The picks below each test a different reason the ICP might care, so the results tell Flowglad something.

| Brief | Callout (who it's for) | Identity (ex-PPC manager) | Revenue (money) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **3A1**, brief hook N2 with an agency callout | **3C1** "You were a great PPC manager…" | **3D1** "…a pay cut you didn't choose." |
| 4 | **4A1**, brief hook N1 with an agency callout | **4C1** "Remember when you checked search terms every Monday?" | **4D1** "…agencies that stop the waste first." |

If Flowglad would rather you use the brief's hooks word for word, swap 3A1 for N2 and 4A1 for N1. The angle being tested stays the same.

**Decision rule (set this before anything goes live):**
- **Primary metric:** 3-second hold rate. That's the hook's whole job.
- **Secondary metric:** comment rate, because the CTA asks for comments.
- **Guardrail:** average view duration must not drop.
- **What counts as a win:** a hook leads by about 20% or more, and does it consistently. Anything closer is a tie, and a tie is a valid result.
- **Controls:** same body, edit, caption and posting window. If the videos run organically, post them one after another at least 7 days apart. If they run as paid ads, an audience split is cleaner. Flowglad reads the numbers from native analytics.
- **Repeat before you trust it:** if the same angle wins on both briefs, lean into it in the next round.

## Production notes (from the Notion brief) and filming tips

**Specs and deliverables *(brief)***
| Item | What we need |
|---|---|
| Format | Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, shot on phone |
| Length | 30 to 45 seconds, plus a 15-second cut (hook, turn, CTA) |
| Hooks | 3 hooks per brief, each filmed separately over the same body |
| Files | A clean version (no captions, no music) and a captioned version |
| Screen moments | Point to the screen or leave space for it; Flowglad supplies screen recordings to cut in |
| Setting | A real desk or home office, natural light, laptop in shot if you can |

So each brief produces: 3 hooks × the full body, and 3 hooks × the 15 s cut. Every one of those comes in both a clean and a captioned version.

**Filming tips (from `talking-head-and-piece-to-camera`)**
- **Setup:** phone at eye level, facing a window, with a mic close to you. Keep your eyes in the top third of the frame and leave space at the side where the screen recording will be cut in.
- **Memorise the map, not the script.** That matches the brief's *"Say it your way, don't read it."* Learn three lines word for word:
  - the hook;
  - the closing line ("Clients who understand the work stay" for Brief 3, "clients put more in" for Brief 4);
  - the CTA.

  Talk through the problem, turn and payoff beats in your own words.
- **Order:** film all 3 hooks back to back while your energy is fresh, then the body once, then the 15 s turn line.
- **Retakes:** retake one beat at a time, never the whole video. A line that fails three times is a writing problem, so rewrite it. Use the take where you sound like yourself.
- **Before sending:** watch each cut on mute and check the on-screen text carries the story on its own.
