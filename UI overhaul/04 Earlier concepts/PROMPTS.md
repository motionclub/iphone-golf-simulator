## 01 / CLUB BUBBLE

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished game UI concept board for Motion Club, a friends-first iPhone party sports game with Wii/Switch Sports clarity and soft plastic appeal. This is an EXPLORATORY UI MOCKUP, not a change to the map. Reference image 1 is the actual tropical court screenshot and is an EDIT TARGET: preserve the environment, its geometry, objects, lighting, ocean, court placement and camera angle exactly. Add only UI over it; do not redraw or improve the world. References 2 and 3 are supporting actual rooftop and volcano maps, only for small accent palettes, never mix their scenery into the main image.
Output high-resolution 2048x1536 landscape design board. Top 1152 px is one 16:9 game screenshot using the unchanged tropical screenshot full bleed. Bottom 384 px is a beautifully typeset warm off-white component comparison strip with generous spacing. It is OUTSIDE the game. It has three columns showing related UI components: a golf landmark bubble reading "WATER CARRY" with smaller "Clear the inlet" and a pointer plus anchor dot; a player introduction plate reading "ADNAN" and small "YOU"; and a pair of compact map-theme examples reading "SKY OPEN" with "ROOFTOP COURT", and "MAGMA OPEN" with "CRATER COURT". Use navy typography and high contrast. No full characters drawn in the component strip.
Main screenshot: a map-arrival title treatment anchored safely bottom-left, 5% from left edge and above 8% bottom safe inset. Exact large title "TROPICAL OPEN", small label "CENTRE COURT", and small descriptor "OCEAN SESSION". All text crisp, well kerned, flat readable sans serif. Keep hierarchy title first, location second, session third, no duplicate category labels. Map stays clearly visible, UI occupies under a quarter of the image. Include a small quiet "Skip intro  ›" pill bottom-right aligned to same safe area. No scoreboard, no serve meter, no joystick, no loading bar, no phone bezel, no extra logo, no fake player portraits. Do not add invented game rules or promises. The border and component sheet label this "CONCEPT" so it is clearly a mockup. Design direction: CLUB BUBBLE. Refined playful bubble, airy warm-white rounded pill housing, large flat navy title, subtly convex soft-plastic finish and a tiny cool shadow. Small lime capsule "CENTRE COURT" tucked just above the title, modest turquoise accent circle with a simple white tennis-ball seam symbol at the left of the pill. Asymmetric single small speech tail makes it a welcome bubble, not a generic menu card. No thick blue outlines, heavy extrusion or sparkles. Size about 42% screen width and 18% screen height. One thin small unboxed navy "OCEAN SESSION" under title. Component family uses same white bubble form, with lime tropical, lavender rooftop and coral magma accents. The golf bubble is compact white with blue inset icon and clean tail; ADNAN white pill with a lime YOU tab. Design board strip heading exact "01 / CLUB BUBBLE", small subtitle "Soft welcome. Quick pop." Keep playful polish and careful optically balanced spacing.
