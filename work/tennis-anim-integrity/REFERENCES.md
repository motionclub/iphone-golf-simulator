## How each fix will use them
- Before re-keying any clip: a reference contact sheet of the matched phases (prep, load, contact, follow-through, recovery) from the
  listed id + timestamps, next to our clip rendered from the same camera angle. GATE_RESULTS.md gets a REF_MATCH line per clip naming
  the ids and timestamps.
- Driver timing fixes (serve pace, swing start, contact re-timing, judder) are checked against the RT clips: the order and relative
  duration of take-back → forward swing → contact → finish, and that the racket is fastest at the ball.
