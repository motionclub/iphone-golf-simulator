# Hair / headwear source inventory — no adoption changes

The strongest existing solid-volume reuse candidates are Unity/Assets/Characters/HeroBase/Models/Hair_Default_Male.fbx and Hair_Default_Female.fbx, with independent Hair/Hair_Default_<Sex>.prefab and Materials/Hair_M.mat/Hair_F.mat. They also exist under ArtDir/hero/base_lock/unity_import. The prefabs contain rigid MeshRenderer parts Hair_M/Hair_F, separate from the complete bald body. Material YAML explicitly uses _Surface0 (opaque), _Cull2. The recorded optional hair triangle counts are Male9824/Female4960. Existing base_lock previews show these volumes on the former original HeroBase assemblies. This proves available editable/source geometry, not their fit to the accepted animated OriginalSeam head.

A modest fitted reuse is plausible for one cut per sex: align each volume to the unchanged accepted head in bind space, preserve the complete scalp/ears/face, and attach rigidly to Head. The current exporter already collects enabled Hair_F as either skinned or rigid hair; it does not equivalently collect Hair_M or arbitrary hairstyle/headwear names. Native rig representation supports rigid parts/bone tracks already, but catalogue selection/tint/resource inclusion and exact native/Unity proofs would still be required. None of that is enabled in this inventory task.

The larger old V5 catalogue lives in Resources/Tennis/Hero/Hero_01_Tennis.prefab: Hair_Default, Ponytail, Bob, Long, Curly, Buzz, Waves, Bald, separate BandFill variants and *_Free variants; Hat and Hat_Visor are also present. HeroTennisDriver.closedHairV5 identifies the old opaque volume approach. They were fitted to the old Hero01 head and are not drop-in compatible with the current bald original. Reusing them would require cut-specific inner-shell/ear/forehead clearance and head-bound transforms, not merely changing HeroKit.Apply's early policy. Cap/sweatband names in HeroKit are menu catalogue entries; this inventory found no independent original-compatible Cap/Sweatband source.

Resources/Hero/golfer_m.fbx / golfer_f.fbx retain Hair_* parts with MakeHuman hair-card textures under Resources/Hero/Hair (Crop, Fringe, Mop, Quiff, SideBob, Bob, Long, Ponytail, Braid). HeroGolfer.Dress explicitly sets hairName=null. These card textures/shader are not suitable to satisfy the locked opaque solid-volume requirement. HeroGolfer's headwear code explicitly says there are no hats yet; its Headwear enum alone is not drawable original-head content.

The current active tennis/native policy intentionally bypasses hair/headwear. HeroKit.Apply applies skin/racket/kit only; native CharacterModelPreview offers the legacy catalogue but MatchHero assets have no adopted hair. The default female rival is therefore bald by policy, not failed source loading. There is useful source for a small ref-fit reuse lane, especially the two independent original HeroBase opaque default cuts, but no measured reference-fit or animated clearance PASS yet. This inventory does not authorize replacing heads, introducing card-based hair, enabling the whole old catalogue, or claiming native resource parity for hair.
