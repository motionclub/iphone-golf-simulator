## The design

- **One style for both heroes.** Large expressive eyes, thick tidy brows, a small simplified nose, soft blush. Today the man is semi-realistic and the woman is cartoon.
- **About six heads tall,** with the head, hands and feet slightly enlarged so they read on a phone.
- **Hair on by default:** a textured crop with a low fade for him, a high ponytail for her. Caps and visors become options worn over the hair.
- **Limbs with simple, clear shapes.** A rounded shoulder, a gentle upper arm, a forearm that is fuller near the elbow and tapers to the wrist. No tube arms, no fine muscle detail.
- **A real low sneaker.** Rounded toe clearly lower than the heel, a waist, a flat thick sole with a coloured outsole line and heel tab.
- **Garments with construction.** Collar and placket, cuff bands, cloth thickness at every hem, crisp pleats with a waistband, a matte knit weave. Her kit is built to the same standard as his.
- **Customisation zones are designed in:** primary colour, accent trim, crest patch, sole and heel tab, headwear.
- **Skin:** satin, warm, with blush. No vinyl gloss.

## What I would change before building from these

- Her legs and arms came out more muscular than I intended, most visibly on the colourway sheet. I would slim them about 15 %.
- His hands and forearms are a touch heavy.
- The crest patch is a blank placeholder for a number or emblem.
