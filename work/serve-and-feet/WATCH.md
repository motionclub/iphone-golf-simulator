## 1. Run — `JJEDwXeDjBc` (feet in frame in every cell used)

**Takes used** (all in the 289–368.5 take unless noted): 300.00–302.50, 305.40–307.60; 392.40–393.50 (368.5–457.5 take). Read as 4x3 sheets of a colour-tracked crop (pink shirt), 1/10, 1/15, 1/30 and 1/60 s apart.

**Feet, in order — a run away from the camera (t=301.70–302.37, 3/4 back view)**
1. Every step is **single support**: one foot is on the court (forefoot / mid-foot first, heel low) while the other is lifted behind with the **heel up toward the buttock**.
2. t=302.20–302.23: left foot pushing off (heel off, toes down), right foot trailing high behind (heel well above the ankle line, toward the buttock).
3. **t=302.27: both feet are off the court** (flight), the two ankles at about the same height, a shoe-height or more above the court. The flight phase is short, **about 1-3 frames at 60 fps (20-50 ms)**; at 1/30 s spacing only this one frame shows both shoes clear.
4. t=302.30: **right foot lands** ahead of the pelvis; the left leg trails behind with the knee bent.
5. t=302.33: right foot flat and bearing weight; left heel high behind.
6. t=302.37: left foot swings forward under the pelvis past the planted right foot (knees close together), and the next step starts.
7. The swing foot always passes the planted foot **with the knee leading**; the planted foot is on the ground about 0.10-0.15 s, the other foot is in the air the rest of the step.
- Step period **0.27-0.33 s (3.0-3.7 steps per second)**, counted over 301.70-302.43; THIN (±0.05 s: the step is shortening because he is about to plant).
- Arms: opposite arm and leg. When the right foot trails, the left arm is forward and up (hand about chest height, elbow bent ~90°) and the right arm back; the racket stays in the right hand, head up and slightly back (racket head at shoulder height behind the right shoulder at 302.37, pointing up and forward at 302.30).
- Trunk leans forward about 10-15°, head level; shoulders turn against the pelvis a little (counter-rotation); the head looks toward the ball side, not straight down the run.

**Recovery after a stroke (t=300.00-301.70)**
1. t=300.00-300.50: forehand finish, **right leg kicks back and up** (open stance), left foot on the ground.
2. t=300.60-300.80: **lands in a wide, low base** (feet about two shoulder-widths apart, knees bent, weight between them, torso turned toward the court centre).
3. t=300.90-301.40: **3-4 small quick steps** (alternating feet, about 0.1 s each; I could not tell which foot moves first), knees bent, racket low in the right hand, torso turning toward the direction of travel.
4. t=301.50-301.70: opens into running strides (section above). He never stands up straight between the stroke and the run.
- The long version (t=305.40-307.60): a stretched run-in, a **lunge** on the outside foot with the other leg trailing (t=306.00-306.20, torso low, both arms out), a push back up (306.40) and wide recovery side-steps (306.60-307.40), the first big step out at 307.60.

**Lateral run (t=392.40-393.50, front 3/4 view, moving toward the viewer's right)**
1. Chest and hips face the camera while he travels to the viewer's right, i.e. they are **not facing the way he moves**: about 45-60° off the travel direction (my reading, ±20°); the head is turned to the ball.
2. Racket carried low in the right hand (viewer's left), head behind the hip.
3. **Step-close steps** with short hops: the leading foot steps wide, the trailing foot follows and lands beside it (does not cross over); the feet stay close to the court between steps, one or both briefly off it (t=392.50-392.70; I could not resolve which at 1/10 s, THIN).
4. t=393.10-393.30: both feet plant wide, knees deeply bent, weight on the outside foot, then the stroke.
- For the longer run-in (301.70-302.37) he turns the body fully to the direction of travel (section above): **a short wide ball is shuffled with the body half turned, a longer one is run with the body facing the way he goes.**

**Could not see / THIN**
- Left versus right foot at each frame when the camera angle hides one shoe (marked "viewer's" or left out); the exact cadence (±0.05 s); the torso lean to better than ±5°.
- A clean side-on lateral run of 3+ full strides: not found. The two lateral reads (301.70-302.37 and 392.40-393.50) are three-quarter views, **THIN on the side-on leg picture**.
- A backward run: not looked for (the game's backing-away case plays the forward run in reverse).

## What this gives the animation (order only)

- **Run:** alternate single-support steps with a short flight phase; heel up to the buttock on the trailing foot; the swing foot passes with the knee leading; opposite arm swing; racket in the right hand, head up; trunk lean 10-15°; ~3.0-3.7 steps per second.
- **Left / right runs:** the body turns toward the direction of travel; a short wide ball is shuffled half-turned, a longer one is run facing the way he goes; step-close steps with a small hop in the shuffle; a wide, low plant at the end.
- **Recovery:** wide low base, then 3-4 small quick steps, then strides.
- **Walk:** one step per 0.5 s, cycle about 1.0 s; heel-first, a shoe-height clearance, no flight; racket hanging low, little arm swing; left arm swings opposite.
- **Serve:** stance with the ball hidden in the left fist (no bounce) → ball lifted and released → trophy with the knees starting to bend → deepest bend with the racket dropped and the pelvis and shoulders coiled → legs extend, hips turn, shoulders and arm follow → both feet leave the court → contact at the top → left foot lands first, right leg kicks back → right foot steps through.
