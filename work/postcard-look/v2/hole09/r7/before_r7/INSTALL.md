## What repair round 2 changed (hole scripts only; libs, textures, Unity files untouched)

Review finding (medium, hole 9, `hole09_3_ruin.png`): "The ruin still reads as stacked bricks. Much better than before (an arch, a ruin footprint), but the piers are plain box courses with flat faces": 8-9 rectangular
courses of near-identical boxes with straight horizontal cut lines, diagonal triangle splits in the shading of every block face ("shear-stretched UVs"), a regular paving-brick pattern on the tops, the arch crown hangs
with a gap to the right pier, its underside is a flat green triangle, the ref ruin is rough broken masonry with ragged tops. Fix hint: chip / bevel / displace each block, break the tops, own UV scale and rotation per
block, box UVs per block, seat the arch ends on the piers, rubble in the gap. The libs and textures were regenerated by the shared fixers before this round (`lib_sha_r6_start.txt` against `lib_sha_round5.txt`: postcard_look_lib,
postcard_props_lib, golf_look_textures, postcard_look_verify changed; Basalt / Lava / Smoke PNGs changed, Masonry_C / _N byte-identical); the hole was rebuilt on them first (`r6/base/`: 16 in-build gates PASS, 241,293 triangles,
every gate of `run_all_gates.sh` as before: the whole `r6/base_gates/` set) and the finding was then fixed in `blender/scripts/hole09_masonry.py` (rewritten), `hole09_look.py` (call sites, `chip_blocks`, ARCH_H_RIGHT) only.

Measured causes (round-1 stage, `r6/face_stats.py`): the "triangle splits" are NOT UV shear (every face's UV is an exact affine map of its plane, residual 0): 20.7 % of the 2,388 masonry faces had a loop normal more than 3 deg (up to 28 deg)
off their own face normal, because the stones were smooth-shaded with a 32 deg crease angle, so a large quad took four different vertex normals and the triangulation showed as a diagonal; the vertical dark streaks on every face
were mortar joints + weathering stains inside the UV windows (the windows were whole brick ROWS); the tops carried the paving pattern because the top UVs were a random tile of the whole brick texture; all stones of a course shared one
bed / top height (the straight lines); the right pier top (3.9 m) was below the highest right wedge (4.2 m), so a wedge stood on it, and the left ring overshot the crown into the air.

1. **Shading** - every stone is FLAT shaded (`StoneSet.flush` -> `shade_flat`): loop normal == face normal (RUIN_FLAT_FACES: 0 of 6,013 polygons over 1 deg on the blend, worst 0.03 deg; FBX re-import worst 0.81 deg). A pillow apex on the faces (the
   review's "chip / bevel / noise-displace") was tried first and REJECTED: the convex hull turns it into a 4-6 triangle pyramid and flat shading shows an X on every face; the stones keep planar faces (tapered, sheared, twisted so no two are parallel)
   and corner chips of 4-13 cm (<= 4 cm on the top of a stone that carries another one).
2. **UV** - every face has its own window inside ONE brick of `Masonry_C.png` (u AND v), so no mortar line crosses any face: the brick interiors are the intersection of a pixel scan and the texture generator's own joint table
   (`golf_look_textures.mat_masonry`, seed 1001; the pixel scan alone missed 5 of its 46 joints); the faces of one stone prefer the same brick (a stone keeps one tone, neighbours differ); tops use a random 90-degree turn and a lower density (0.26 x); 0.3-1.5 x the
   contract density, UV_DENSITY PASS. RUIN_UV_BRICK_INTERIOR re-derives the joints from the generator's random tables and measures every polygon (round-1 stage 24.5 %, now >= 95 %).
3. **Layout** - skyline rubble on wavy bed levels (`lay_wall`): each wall has 16 bed levels (27-44 cm apart, a 1.5-4 cm wave of 7-14 m wavelength, a 7-12 cm jog at one place for 70 % of the levels); a stone's top lands on one of them (+-1.5 cm) and it spans 1, 2 or 3 levels
   (0.27-0.85 m high, 0.34-2.4 m long: 24 % short, 16 % long), so neighbouring stones of a course share a bed within the run tolerance (running bond) but no bed is a ruler line; a bed rests on the highest cell of its run (hollow <= 4.5 cm + the tilt / chip of the stone below; corner chips on the top of a stone that carries another are <= 3 cm; a 3 cm bed sink was tried and reverted, see item 8); depth
   0.86-1.0 of the wall with a random out-of-line face (relief); joints keep >= 12 cm off the joints below where the run allows; the doorway keeps its quoin rows (exact top) and ONE lintel stone bearing 0.55 m on each jamb; the first row is buried 0.25-0.45 m.
4. **Tops** - the last stone of a column is cut to the seeded broken profile (full stretches, stubs, collapsed gaps) with a sloped, chipped top; nothing is ever laid on a broken top.
5. **Arch** - left pier 6.2 m, right pier 4.4 m (was 3.9: the right wedges are now INSIDE the pier, nothing stands on it); 11 wedges, ONE crown wedge (6) missing and lying in a rubble heap with 9 half-buried stones + 4 resting on them under the gap
   (round 1: two missing, a cantilevered crown that ended in the air); wedges are planar flat-shaded hulls (the "flat green triangle" underside of the round-1 crown was a smooth-shaded intrados triangle whose UV window sampled a moss patch; now every wedge face is flat with its own brick-interior window), chips <= 8 cm; the piers are rubble stacks of 0.34-1.0 m stones with ragged ends.
6. **Fallen blocks** - the 13 `DRESS_BLOCK` instances take 4 rough, rounded (p-norm 5), subdivided, +-1 cm displaced, flat-shaded variants per kind (98 welded vertices, 192 triangles, brick-interior UVs) instead of one chipped cuboid per kind.
7. Numbers: 341 masonry stones in 12 DRESS_RUIN_ / DRESS_ARCH_ meshes (209 + 41 wall stones, 24 wall-foot rubble stones, 3 refused by the shore / route clip), 13 DRESS_BLOCK instances (was 219 stones + 13 blocks). Triangles 244,825 (limit 250,000; round-1 stage 241,197, rebuilt-on-new-libs baseline 241,293); FBX 4.77 MB (limit 20).
8. Gates (`tools/ruin_check.py`, read the finished mesh only, run on the blend AND the FBX re-import): the five of round 1 (RUIN_SUPPORTED, RUIN_LINTEL_BEARING, RUIN_BASE_BURIED, RUIN_NOT_BOXES, RUIN_UV_WINDOWS) + five new ones: RUIN_FLAT_FACES, RUIN_UV_BRICK_INTERIOR,
   RUIN_BEDS_NOT_RULED, RUIN_TOPS_RAGGED, RUIN_ARCH_SEATED. Four of the five new ones FAIL on the round-1 stage (measured on r6/base with the same tool: FLAT_FACES 766 polygons over 3 deg, UV_BRICK_INTERIOR 24.5 %, TOPS_RAGGED 38 % tilted, ARCH_SEATED gap 27 deg; BEDS_NOT_RULED passed it at its limit, 35 %) and all PASS now. Changes to the measuring code (disclosed, none
   relaxes a threshold): RUIN_NOT_BOXES measures the stone length along the stone's own axis and ALSO keeps the round-1 bounding-box definition (both need >= 0.25: a stone yawed 30-45 deg inflated the box with its thickness); the support sample cross reaches 8 cm
   (was 3 cm: a joint between two stones is a 5 cm gap plus the chips of both), and a bed point inside another stone's convex hull counts as supported (needed only by the 3 cm bed sink that was tried and reverted: the round-1 ray test called 94 stones floating on it; kept because it is exact for convex stones).    Old thresholds (>= 2 sample points / 50 % of the bed / lintel 50 % at each end) are unchanged.
