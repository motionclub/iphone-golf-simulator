# Hole 8 Needle: library issues found while building (2026-10-04)

Nothing in the foundation files (postcard_look_lib.py, postcard_props_lib.py, postcard_look_verify.py, postcard_lib.py, golf_look_textures.py, Look PNGs, Unity) was edited.
Every item below is worked around INSIDE `blender/scripts/hole08_look.py` / `hole08_build.py`. Repro = the call that shows it, on the hole 8 scene built by `hole08_build.py`.
Lib sha256 at the final run: `logs/lib_sha_final.txt`.

Not bugs but worth knowing: the library's `LK_CLIFF` skin keeps a 0.15-0.45 m grass fringe above the cap ledge (CRATER_WALL_COVERED reports 19 % of rays on Needle as information, not a gate there);
`L.build_path` buries ~6 % of the path where it crosses a 6-14 cm ground step (it dips under the higher surface instead of rising over a ball): the two Needle paths cross rough / first cut / fairway
boundaries at an angle, so the buried share is 6 % of their area (lib `PATH_UNDER_BALL`: max 1.15 cm over the collider, ball hidden <= 3.5 %).
