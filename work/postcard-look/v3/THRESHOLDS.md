# Fixed thresholds — recorded before any v3 build measurement
No goal threshold is calibrated or loosened. The values below are supplied by goal15, or inherited from the existing proof metrics before any v3 build.
- Fairway mow luminance gap: (light-band mean Y - dark-band mean Y) / light-band mean Y <= 0.12. Green <= 0.08. Renderer-masked, world-band samples exclude edges/objects/shadows; raw samples retained. No usable samples = FAIL. Before any v3 Game-view band measurement, validity support is fixed at >=512 pixels per authored plateau after 1px erosion and controlled original-vs-shadow-disabled exclusion; missing support fails.
- Sway colours/caps: existing verifier + GolfPlantSwayCheck unchanged; root R <= .05, B <= .02, A >= .98; caps tuft .06yd, flower .05yd, shrub/tree .03yd, vine .08yd.
- Basalt joints: existing proof thresholds unchanged: glow coverage <=20%; non-glow median Y<=55/255, mean S<=.25; vertical line share<=80%; horizontal rungs<=20%; >=3 junction clusters/100 skeleton pixels. Raw component and skeleton data retained.
- New columns: 5 or 6 sides, across width .6–1.8m, varied flat/chipped tops.
- Stack height/base-width >=3.0; no object scale or anisotropy >2.0; old per-mesh rock verifier unchanged.
- Split cliffs: BOTH islands >=80% projected outline with visible cliff face and nearby broken surf. Renderer masks and full aerial retained. Before any course-outline measurement, raster support is fixed at 2px Euclidean adjacency from every frozen projected top-outline pixel to actual visible cliff pixels and 6px from frozen sea-level base-outline pixels to actual visible SURF pixels. All hidden backsides and out-of-frame outline pixels remain in the cliff denominator; samples are not clipped away. SURF must be present at the base; its 6px support does not impose 80% foam coverage or alter the inherited foam cap. Brokenness is inspected separately with retained masks and component data.
- Ruin: <=20% of disconnected pieces are unbroken axis-aligned cuboids. Piece count and geometric classification retained.
- Gameplay framing: each of six fixed address frames must show a fairway-side cliff drop; sea/lava >=15% of frame (216,000px at 900x1600); visible landmark extent >=4% height (64px). Material masks alone cannot stand in for landmark masks.
- Hole triangle cap <=300,000; existing verifier's tighter 250,000 triangle cap is not altered. Tee draw calls <=250. SetPass reported without inventing a cap.
- Scoring: exact vertices/faces/marker transforms vs baseline, max vertex delta 0.000m. No tolerance can confer PASS.
- Historical texture gates and historical library fingerprints remain unchanged. If newly required appearance conflicts with a historical gate, preserve and report that FAIL; never relabel it PASS.
