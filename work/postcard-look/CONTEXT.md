## What the game renders today (the problem)
* `Unity/Assets/Scripts/Course/HoleView.cs` `BuildFromModel`: every renderer material whose name is in `Palette` is replaced by `Mat(color)` = URP/Lit with `_BaseColor` only and `_Smoothness` 0.1.
  Names not in `Palette` (e.g. `MAT_LAVA`) keep whatever the FBX importer made. Only meshes named `TERRAIN*|FAIRWAY*|GREEN*|TEE_BOX*|BUNKER*|CART_PATH*` get MeshColliders; `GroundHeight` raycasts from y=400 down, miss = 0 (water).
  World units are YARDS (model metres x 1.0936). Tee marker lands on course (0,0) = world origin; X right, Z down the hole. Water is world y = 0, play surface y = 6 m = 6.56 yd.
* `GolfGame.Awake`: one directional light 1.1, flat grey ambient (.55,.6,.65), linear fog 250-700 (.75,.85,.95), camera clear = Skybox but no skybox set, no fill, no post.
* `CameraRig`: FOV 60, near .05, far 900, `FrameAddress` = 4.5 yd behind the ball, 2.4 yd up, looking 1.5 yd ahead (putts 3.2 / 1.4 / 2.5). The phone camera therefore sees mostly the near play surface in the lower two thirds and the horizon in the top fifth. Ground texture quality at 2-20 yd and the horizon/sky/fog dominate the look.
* Active URP asset (all quality levels but one) = `Unity/Assets/Resources/Tennis/Rendering/TennisURP.asset`: HDR on, MSAA 4, main-light shadow map 2048, shadow distance 42 m, additional lights per-pixel, limit 2 per object. Do not edit it (another chat edits it).
* Baseline FBXs (`work/postcard-look/baseline/`): flat `MAT_*` meshes; rocks are one 12-vertex icosahedron instanced; `WATER_OCEAN` = 4 verts / 1 polygon; `MAT_FOAM` strips; hole 10 `WATER_LAVA` = 1 polygon; no plants/path/arch masonry/waterfall.
* The "before" in-game stills (`ArtDir/screenshots/golf_postcards/hole0N_*.jpg`) were made by `Unity/Assets/Editor/PostcardCameraStills.cs` (Play mode, `CameraRig.FrameAddress`, 900x1600 RenderTexture, `GolfGame.StartHole(i)` via reflection, Canvas disabled). Env var `GOLF_STILLS_OUT` sets the output dir. Reuse that approach; it renders the real game camera.

## Tennis bar — copy TECHNIQUES, never objects, never edit
* Ground: `TennisLook.StyleIsland` (`TennisLook.cs` ~line 22): URP Lit, `_BaseMap` + `_BumpMap` (`_NORMALMAP` on), `_Smoothness` .12. Texture import: `TennisCharacterTextureImporter` (normal map type, aniso 4, compressed, no crunch).
* Plants: `TennisEnvironmentV4.cs` — one palette-atlas URP Lit material with `enableInstancing`, `_BaseColor` .94 grey, `_Smoothness` .12; props 320-500 faces; prefab spawn + fit-by-height.
* Ocean: `Unity/Assets/Resources/Tennis/Shaders/TennisWater.shader` = `GolfArcade/TennisWater`: props `_Shallow`, `_Deep`, `_Sky`, `_DeepDistance` (60), `_WaveScale` (.35), `_Sparkle` (6). World-space only (no UV needed): colour = lerp(Shallow, Deep, saturate((length(positionWS.xz) - 20) / _DeepDistance)), animated swell normals, fresnel to sky, sun sparkle (needs a sun). NO foam, no vertex colour, no alpha. Distance is from the world origin (= the golf tee), not from the shore. Used by tennis through `new Material(shader)` (`TennisLook.StyleArena`).
* Sky: `TennisLook.LightScene`: `Tennis/Shaders/TennisSky` (`GolfArcade/TennisSky`: `_Panorama`, `_Horizon`, `_Zenith`, `_Sea`, `_SunDir`, `_SunColor`, `_Arc` 180, `_Heading`, `_Exposure`) with `Tennis/Environment/SkyPanorama.png` (bright blue sky, warm cumulus, glow on the left) as `RenderSettings.skybox`; sun key `SunColor (1,.90,.76)` intensity 2.3, soft shadows strength .72, bias .05/.4; cool fill directional `(.84,.90,1)` intensity .8 no shadows; trilight ambient sky (.42,.58,.86) / equator (.66,.58,.50) / ground (.24,.26,.22); linear fog colour (.80,.84,.90) 140..900. Post: `TennisLook.SetupPost(camera)` → `Resources.Load<VolumeProfile>("Tennis/Rendering/TennisPost")` cloned into a global Volume (priority 10) + `UniversalAdditionalCameraData.renderPostProcessing = true` (ACES, bloom, warm grade, vignette). `LightScene` also spawns `HeroRimLight` (hero-only): do NOT call `LightScene` from golf; reimplement the settings in `GolfAtmosphere`.
* Volcano: `TennisVenueBuilder.Volcano.cs`: generated basalt albedo + rock normal + crack mask + crust + crust glow + lavafall glow (runtime `Tex(...)` with Fbm/TileNoise); lighting `Light()`: sun (1,.52,.26) 2.3, trilight sky (.34,.24,.40) / equator (.55,.30,.22) / ground (.60,.22,.08) "the lava lights everything from below", fog (.42,.19,.14), extra orange directionals; sky = dark smoky dome with glowing undersides.
* Quality bar images: `ArtDir/screenshots/venues/{resort,volcano}_2_postcard.png` (painted sky, warm light, bloom, textured stone/basalt with glowing cracks, instanced shrubs/palms).

## Hole facts
* Course.Postcards(): index 0/1/2 = hole 8 Needle / 9 Split / 10 Crater (`Number` 8/9/10). Design data `blender/scripts/hole0N_design.py` (course yards; Blender metres = yards x 0.9144, tee at Blender (0,0), +Y down the hole). `SCENERY` dict is free-form and read only by the build scripts (extend it freely; `python3 blender/scripts/postcard_emit_cs.py --check Unity/Assets/Scripts/Course/Hole.cs` must still say no drift).
* Needle: 3 pads (tee 1,745 yd², middle 2,378, green 1,635) strung on an S-curve, 2 water ellipses cut the necks, 1 bunker. Split: C-shaped ring — dry ridge (left) + ribbon (right) joined at both ends, 1 water ellipse in the ribbon gap, 3 bunkers. Crater: tee/rim strip + a pin pillar (TOP at fairway height), 1 lava water ellipse (112 x 76 yd) between them, 2 bunkers, far basalt wall = ring of rock instances 295-355 yd from the crater centre, 11 basalt stacks in the lava.
* Pipeline: `blender/scripts/hole0N_build.py` + `postcard_lib.py` (`start`, `build_terrain` [slots 0 ROUGH top, 1 CLIFF, 2 CLIFF_DARK; walls vertical/undercut], `build_play_surfaces`, `build_water`, `import_rocks`, `place_rock`, `build_arch`, `build_ruin_wall`, `scatter_rocks`, `build_gameplay`, `export_fbx`, `save`). Gates: `blender/scripts/postcard_verify.py` (mesh gates on the blend + re-imported FBX), `blender/scripts/postcard_check.py` (pure-Python LieAt mirror), `Tools/PostcardCheck/run.sh` (compiles the real Hole.cs/CourseShot.cs), `Unity/Assets/Editor/PostcardImportCheck.cs` (`GolfArcade.EditorTools.PostcardImportCheck.Run`, 44 gates incl. ground height band, markers, palette override counts, lava colour — the last two WILL need updating for LK materials; never weaken the ground/lie/marker gates), `Unity/Assets/Tests/EditMode/PostcardHoleTests.cs`. Read `blender/scripts/POSTCARDS_README.md` before touching Blender.
* Colliders are single-sided: no upward-facing face of a TERRAIN/FAIRWAY/GREEN/... mesh outside the top outline or inside a water ellipse; walls vertical or undercut. Visual skins/cladding are `ROCK_*` and may be anything.
* The visible cliff height is only ~6 m (play surface 6 m above the sea, walls go to -6). Dramatic height in the stills must come from NON-playable scenery: tall sea stacks, distant islands, background cone, wall ring. The playable islands stay 6 m.
