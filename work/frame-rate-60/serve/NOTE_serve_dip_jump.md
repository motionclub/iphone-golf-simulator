## What the clip does now (clip seconds; the prefab times 0.2 / 0.7 / 1.3333 / contact 1.7 are unchanged)
* 0.20–0.37  **fold**: pelvis sits back (0.30 m) and down (0.17 m), pelvis pitch 9 -> 32 deg, chest rounds 8 deg more, knees ~60 deg; arms hang, the racket hangs outside the right thigh. Head keeps looking at the court.
* 0.37–0.47  **pop**: legs and trunk extend together, hips go up and a little forward, the racket arm swings up in front of the right shoulder (elbow low, forearm forward-up, hoop rising).
* 0.47–0.67  **both feet off the court** (toes leave at frame 14, back down at frame 21), hips up to +0.225 m above the ready stance (feet 15 cm clear), trunk arched, toss arm climbing, racket hoop over the handle by frame 16 (0.53 s, ball at the head).
* 0.70       touch-down on the toes = ball release; knees absorb, then the slow load into the trophy.
* 1.33–1.50  trophy -> **racket drop**: elbow out to the right at shoulder height and behind the shoulder line, forearm falls behind the back, the racket hangs behind the shoulder blades (never in front of the chest).
* 1.50–1.70  drive: the elbow leads up and forward, the racket comes up from the right side to the (unchanged) contact pose; contact hop, landing and follow-through as before except the follow-through arm keeps its elbow outside the shoulder (no flip) and the racket clears the left thigh.
* Left (toss) arm: its palm is put on the path the game gives the ball (`TennisServeRoutine`: straight line stance -> release, smoothstep over the wind-up; then 0.2 s to the trophy palm; then fixed in hero space), computed on the solved chest each frame, two passes so the palm at stance / release / trophy is the one the driver will measure (<= 2 mm). Elbow flexion peaks ~143 deg at the deepest fold (hand tucked at the chest), opens to straight at the release. After the trophy it comes down on the LEFT side only.

## Things found on the way (do not repeat)
* A deep trunk fold with the ball held at the belt puts the folded shoulder 10-20 cm from the game's ball line: the toss arm cannot reach. What worked: hips pushed well back (0.30 m), moderate trunk fold, the pop one frame earlier, and the ORIGINAL stance ball position (a more extended stance arm makes the elbow snap 70 deg in one frame near full extension). The left wrist floor was lowered to 0.30 arm lengths (elbow up to ~146 deg) in this clip only.
* Right-wrist twist basins: asking the racket for poses the wrist can only reach in the other basin flipped the hand 80-100 deg in one frame (also in the job-start clip at frames 23/24 and 53/54). Fixed by (a) hand paths that keep the hand >= 25 cm from the shoulder, (b) a prescribed twist schedule (-7 -> -83 deg over the windup), (c) an elbow-direction hint while the elbow is meant to be out.
* The game draws the racket 1.3x about the grip and the heroes wear the kit: the Blender checks must include both (Racket_Classic.scale 1.3 in the preview copy only; the exported FBX racket stays 1.0).
* `AssistContact` only acts within +-0.16 s of contact, so the drop is what the clip says. Per-row jumps of 0.4-0.7 m of hand/strings at clip 1.53-1.66 in the trace are the driver's trophy->contact time warp (u^1.35), not clip pops.
