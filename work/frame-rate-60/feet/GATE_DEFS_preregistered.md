# GATE DEFINITIONS — written BEFORE the clips were made (2026-10-03)

Every gate is a number or a yes/no that a script computes from files. Thresholds are fixed here and are not changed after a result is seen. If a threshold turns out wrong the gate is reported FAIL with the number, not loosened.
Reply contract: `GATE: SERVE_AND_FEET PASS` only if all ten lines below pass; otherwise `FAIL` with the failed lines only.

Terms. **Floor** = the lowest foot-mesh vertex of that hero in its ReadyIdle frame 0 (both feet flat). **Foot vertices** = body-mesh vertices whose largest skin weight is LeftFoot / LeftToes (left foot) or RightFoot / RightToes (right foot). **Foot height** at a frame = lowest foot vertex of that foot (evaluated, deformed mesh, Blender) minus the floor. **Planted** = foot height <= 10 mm. **Clear** = foot height >= the threshold of the gate. **Cycle** = the loop length of the clip. Authoring frame: r = hero's right, f = forward.

3. **SERVE_HIPS.** Hip yaw = rotation of the Hips bone about the vertical, relative to the Root, + = turned to the hero's right (clockwise from above), computed from the baked bone world matrices. `load` = from frame 0 (and from the stance time) to the frame of deepest knee bend. **Pass: yaw(deepest) - yaw(frame 0) >= 25 deg AND yaw(deepest) - yaw(stance time) >= 25 deg AND the unwind from the deepest bend to contact >= 40 deg**, male and female. Also measured in Play mode on the hero's own Hips transform (stance versus load versus contact); a Unity number below 25 deg fails the gate too.
4. **SERVE_CONTACT.** (a) `checks.json` names the contact time (seconds, to the frame) and the baked action has a `Contact` pose marker on that frame; the racket sweet spot at that frame is the highest point of the sweet spot in the clip within 0.2 s of it (the contact is where the arm is up). (b) The prefab slot carries the same contact time. (c) In Play mode, over at least 12 player serves and 12 rival serves (both sexes appear), at the instant the game reports the serve contact the clip time of Serve equals the named contact within 1 frame (0.034 s) for **every** serve, and the strings-to-ball gap at the player's contact is <= 0.12 m for at least 90 % of the serves and <= 0.20 m for all. The previous Serve clip's numbers from `work/hero-mainstay/proof/contact_lands.txt` are quoted next to the new ones.
5. **WALK_STEPS.** Walk loop, both sexes. (a) Cycle 1.0-1.3 s, last frame == first frame (max bone-quaternion difference < 1e-6, hips location < 1e-6 m). (b) In each cycle each foot is planted for at least 35 % of the frames and clear (foot height >= 40 mm) for at least 20 % of the frames, and its peak height is >= 60 mm. (c) At every frame at least one foot is planted (a walk has no flight). (d) The planted foot's speed relative to the Root during its planted frames is within +-8 % of the clip's design speed (the speed the game scales the clip by), i.e. no sliding when played at its design speed. (e) Play mode: in the walk frames of the proof the planted-toe slide `HeroTennisDriver.FootSkate` has a median < 0.15 m/s and the Walk mixer weight is >= 0.9.
6. **RUN_BOTH_LEGS.** RunForward, RunLeft, RunRight, both sexes. (a) Closed loop as in 5a, cycle 0.50-0.80 s. (b) In every cycle **each foot is planted at least once and clear (foot height >= 60 mm) at least once**, and each foot's peak height is >= 90 mm; the lower of the two peaks is >= 70 % of the higher. (c) Neither foot is planted for more than 55 % of the frames. (d) The planted foot's speed relative to the Root is within +-8 % of the design speed in every planted frame (no skating at the design speed). (e) RunRight faces right and RunLeft faces left: pelvis yaw >= +50 deg / <= -50 deg relative to the Root (the body faces the way it runs) and the foot travels backwards along that direction. RunLeft is its own clip (its frames are not the RunRight frames with the sign flipped; the racket stays in the right hand in both). (f) Play mode: in the proof run, for each foot the lowest-vertex height trace shows >= 60 mm clearance in every cycle of the visible run.
