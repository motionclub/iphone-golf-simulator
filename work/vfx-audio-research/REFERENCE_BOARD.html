Ten lessons we're taking

The full list of 24 patterns with sources is in RESEARCH.md, Part D.

  1. One event, every channel, one timestamp. Celeste dash; Feel MMF_Player
  2. Quality changes the timbre, not just the volume. Matchpoint, PGA 2K, Loughborough golf-feel studies
  3. The controller plays only your own hit, never a TV copy. Wii Remote speaker; Iwata Asks, Super Mario Galaxy
  4. Haptic and sound keep the same tempo, scaled by impact. Apple WWDC19 810
  5. Each tier adds a channel; the top tier stays rare. Mario Tennis Aces ladder; Fever review
  6. The trail carries the grade in flight. Aces shot colours; Switch Sports red serve
  7. Celebrate outcomes, not inputs; make failures funny. Mario Golf Nice Shot; Everybody's Golf miscue
  8. Duck the bed, never boost the hit; music never masks hits. Rocket League HDR; Switch Sports
  9. Surfaces sound different and leave marks. TopSpin 2K25 clay/grass
  10. Budget particles by copies on screen; overdraw is the limit. Azur Games 2026; Apple TBDR
