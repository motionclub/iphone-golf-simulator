## Why the jaw still looked rough (measured root causes)
F2 removed the fake jawline and the clamped hacks, but four smaller things were still left in the lower face:
1. **Jaw contour not the plate's.** The front-view jaw contour of F2 (analytic surface) was **+2.5…+4 mm too wide** over z 1.446–1.466 and **−2…−3.5 mm too narrow** at z 1.49–1.494 (mesh slices: rms 1.44 mm vs a cubic fit of the plate silhouette, which itself fits the raw plate rows to 0.36 mm rms). The contour slope therefore dipped to 0.33 at z 1.47 and rose again to 0.44 (plate: monotone 1.15 → 0.45). That wiggle is the "diagonal band" in profile / 3/4: the normal along the visible jaw wall went −43° → −19° in 10 mm, then −22°, −15°, −9° (`proof/F3_jaw_wall_normal_elevation.png`, red).
2. **C1-only construction.** F1's loft exponent `nf(z)` is piecewise linear with slope jumps at z 1.47 and 1.53 (horizontal creases across the cheek), the jaw-underside blend used the quadratic smooth-min (continuous slope but a curvature jump where the blend starts) and every zone / neck / floor weight was a cubic smoothstep (a weight times a displacement adds a curvature jump at the ramp ends).
3. **Mesh normals, not the surface.** The lower-face mesh has 2–18 mm irregular faces (under-chin, jaw wall); the shading normals computed from it differ from the true surface normals by mean 3.0°, p90 6.7°, p99 16.5°, max 20.8° for F2 (F3 mesh: 3.0 / 6.8 / 17.3 / 22.8; `data/jaw_audit.json`) — the mottled/creased look in 3/4, even where the geometry is fine. Relaxing the mesh made it worse (tried, rejected); custom normals at the vertices fix it without moving any geometry.
4. (Lips) the paint was not wrong in size — it was wrong in *shape and tone*, see below.

## What F3 changes
**Jaw (2,130 Body_F vertices, max 5.15 mm, same zone rules as F2; 2,138 differ from F2, max 2.73 mm, mean 0.59 mm)**
- Contour refit: loft half-width row `a(z)` (cubic B-spline correction, ≤ 5.4 mm, smooth), bowl `cxb` 6.0 → 6.45, blend radius `k1` 0.028 → 0.033, so the **analytic** jaw contour follows the plate's smooth silhouette to rms 0.24 mm; above the jaw it continues monotonically into the skull contour (target: slope 0.55 at z 1.48 decaying to 0.11, meeting the skull at z 1.53). **Mesh** contour vs the plate fit (z 1.452–1.490): F1 rms 1.14 / F2 **1.44** / F3 **0.74 mm** (max 2.17 / 2.20 / 1.51) — `proof/F3_jaw_contour_vs_plate.png`.
- The row correction widens the skull rows by up to 5.4 mm at z 1.48–1.50 (it compensates the blend's pull-in); the real surface moves ≤ 1.9 mm at z 1.50–1.52 and ≤ 0.8 mm at z 1.52–1.56 (zone weights).
- `nf(z)` Gaussian-smoothed (σ 10 mm, |Δn| ≤ 0.05); jaw underside blend = exponential smooth-min (C∞, same peak depth); all zone / neck / floor / chain-fade weights quintic (C2); zone top 1.540 → 1.565 (F2 1.500 → 1.525) with the eye / ear / nostril / mouth loops frozen.
- **Analytic shading normals** on the jaw (custom normals, per loop, blended in by the zone weight): 5,052 loops changed > 0.5°, max change 14.5° vs the old auto normals. Survives the FBX round trip (re-import: max 0.027°, 0 loops > 1°). Not a tilt/bend effect: they are the normals of the smooth analytic surface the vertices were slid onto.
- Wall normal along the visible jaw (analytic): F2 non-monotone (−17° at z 1.470, −26° at 1.482, −10° at 1.495); F3 one smooth roll (−46° → −7°) with a ≤ 2° plateau at −31° over z 1.470–1.482.
- Tried and dropped: larger blend radii (k1 0.045 / 0.058, contour refit each) — the profile step did not move and the jaw angle grew a knob in 3/4; relaxing the mesh tangentially (normals got worse); an `nf` dip.

**Lips (Face_F paint rebuilt, skin geometry unchanged)** — measured from the front plate (`tools/lip_measure.py`: redness R−G > 55 + luminance minimum per column):
- Plate borders at the centre: upper lip 6.8 mm, lower 12.8 mm, flat top border (z 1.4826) falling toward the corners, lower lip ends on the mouth line at |x| ≈ 28 mm.
- Found: F1's upper-lip paint had a **2.6–3.5 mm dome** at the centre (the mouth chain `LOOP_lip_up` peaks there on the philtrum base; paint followed it) and the lower-lip paint a 2 mm **notch** at the centre (chain wobble); lower lip tone was lighter than the skin (ratio 1.05/1.05/1.05) where the plate's is pinker and darker (0.98/0.89/0.87 mid, 0.88/0.78/0.72 low) — that is why it did not read.
- New strips (`tools/lips3.py`): upper lip with a soft cupid's bow on the flat plate border, full lower lip (12.9 mm centre, rounder ends than the plate's thresholded border), mouth line 1.4 mm → corners, all ray-projected onto the final skin (0.32–0.88 mm above it), **pillow normals** on the paint (strip's own geometric normal low-passed + a designed vertical tilt: lower lip +16° top → −26° bottom, upper lip −18° → +9°, fading toward the corners) so the lips have light-top / shaded-bottom form like the plate.
- Tones (render ÷ skin, plate in brackets): upper lip 0.83/0.69/0.65 [0.84/0.69/0.63], lower top 1.05/0.96/0.97 [1.05/0.97/0.99], mid 1.04/0.93/0.94 [0.98/0.89/0.87], low 0.87/0.75/0.74 [0.88/0.78/0.72], mouth line 0.80/0.67/0.66 [0.79/0.62/0.57] (`logs/lip_tones.txt`). F2 was 0.82/0.68/0.65 on the upper lip but **1.05/1.05/1.05 (mid) and 1.04/1.01/1.01 (low)** on the lower lip.
- Geometry of the skin under the lips was **not** changed: the sagittal silhouette at the lips is already 1–2.5 mm *forward* of the plate (z 1.466: −126.0 vs −123.5 mm), so extra protrusion would break the profile; fullness comes from paint shape, tone and shading.
