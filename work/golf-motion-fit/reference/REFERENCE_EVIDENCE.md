Golf motion references and authoring evidence

No detailed reverse-overlap close-up was established from the putting video. Overlap and reverse-overlap exceptions are independently fitted to the hero hand geometry and require close-up visual acceptance. Hand markers alone do not prove anatomical grip quality.

Bunker, half-swing, abort/recovery, aiming, waggle, in-place locomotion, pickup, tee placement and reactions are authored variations, not claims of frame-for-frame correspondence to unseen footage. Their manifest entries distinguish authored motion from inspected swing references.

[Apple's SCNMorpher calculation contract](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/scenekit/scnmorphercalculationmode) specifies that additive targets are added to the base. Golf's SceneKit export therefore stores garment position and normal deltas; FBX retains its own target representation. Tennis's existing exporter and fabric encoding are preserved.
