Majestic Course Atlas

Target images for rolling chat 9’s Majestic look out to every golf hole, from every camera a player sees, plus one target per weather effect. Each target is an AI repaint of a real in-game frame. Routing, greens, hazards and the golfer stay where they are; look, light, dressing and sky change. Flip any frame to today’s game with its button, or all of them with the switch.

Weather

Each effect at a representative hole or venue, built on that place’s approved clear target. Today’s weather is a full-screen grey or yellow veil with speckled turf, and costs +3–4 ms per golf frame. The targets show wet gloss, puddles, storm walls, fog banks in low ground, wind you can see, snow on branches and lava glowing through ash, while the ball, flag and players stay readable. Where a today frame exists it is linked (often from a different hole).

The plan, in short

Make room first

  1. Golf is over budget on every hole: 1.4–5.5 M triangles per view against ≤ 1.0 M. Shadow casters, LOD/impostors and a hole-12 diet come before any new dressing.
  2. Weather moves inside the materials: wetness, snow, ash and fog become shader terms, plus one rain/snow layer. Target: Δ ≤ 1 ms, down from +3–4 ms.

Then build, course by course

  1. Extend chat 9’s self-attaching Majestic layer from 5 holes to all 16, with new files only.
  2. Per hole: a landmark in every direction, clustered dressing that is dense near the corridor and thins outward, shore bands and foam on every island edge, and caldera walls on 21–23.
  3. Order: make-room + weather in parallel, then Cliffside → Wild Isles → Magma Open. At most two heavy builders at once (heavy.sh).

Gates at every camera

  • REF_MATCH against these targets, at the same 5 views.
  • NO_EMPTY_VIEW and NO_VOID at all ~26 sampled cameras per hole, including off-line lies, putts from three sides, the flyover and the live ball cams.
  • PERF_WORST_VIEW: ≤ 1.0 M triangles and ≥ 58 fps at 1080p at each hole’s heaviest view and weather. READABILITY for the ball and flag.

Three decisions for Adnan

  1. Holes 10 and 16 are on Magma Open but draw rain and thunderstorm. Switch them to ash, eruption and smoke? (Recommended; the targets show them that way.)
  2. Keep one signature hour per course: Cliffside morning, Wild Isles golden afternoon, Magma pre-dawn?
  3. Tennis weather: during play as today, or around play (arrival, between games, after the match)?
