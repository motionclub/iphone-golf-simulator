# Hold to swing: what to try on the phone (not run in this pass)

Try it on both controllers. With a TV connected, Unity's own phone sheet is in front. Without a TV, the SwiftUI controller is in front.

1. Line up a drive, then press and HOLD the yellow HOLD TO SWING button. It turns amber (HOLD STILL). Keep the phone still for about half a second: it turns green (SWING!) with one firm tap. Swing while still holding. The ball goes.
2. Hold, take the club back, then lift your thumb at the top of the backswing. The golfer returns to the address, nothing is hit, you feel a warning buzz, and the TV says "Let go — swing reset". The button is yellow again.
3. A quick tap (press and lift at once) does nothing except the reset buzz. No stroke is spent.
4. Hold and swing, letting go right as you hit (thumb off at contact). The ball must still go: there is a 0.2 s grace once the club is on its way down. Note it if a shot ever resets when you let go at contact.
5. Slide your thumb across the glass mid-swing without lifting it. The swing must NOT reset: only lifting lets go.
6. While holding, touch the aim pad or a club arrow with your other thumb. The swing resets as before, and you lift and hold again to swing.
7. Putts: the same steps 1–3 with the putter. While the putt is lined up, the TV shows the ruler on the green (4 / 14 / 32 / 58 ft at 25/50/75/100 % on the flat). As the putter comes back an amber bar runs out with its feet, and the marks it passes turn yellow.
8. Grip check: can you keep a thumb on the 108 pt button (SwiftUI) or the 250 px button (Unity sheet) through a full swing with your normal grip? If not, the button may need to move or grow (follow-up).
9. Online (a guest's phone): the hold and the release stay on that phone, like Start Swing did. Check that the guest's swing still goes to the host.
