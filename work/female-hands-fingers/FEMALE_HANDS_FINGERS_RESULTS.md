## What was wrong (measured)

* The limbs hand was an SDF capsule stack (mitten of equal-width fingers, stub thumb), palm turned 55 deg from a front/back-only silhouette fit.
* **Plate pose** (left-profile plate zoomed with a 1 cm grid, bald/sheet crops): the palm faces **medially** (finger row runs front-to-back: four strips y -6.0 ... +0.1 cm, nearly vertical), fingers are cupped toward the palm, the thumb hangs on the medial side and is **hidden behind the index finger in the lateral view**, the whole hand sits ~3 cm forward of our rings. The old hand straddled y = 0 (band -36 ... +33 mm), the plate hand spans -60 ... +5 mm.
* The wrist rings are 57-60 mm round circles (arms FORM locked them), and the -x ring is 2.7 cm higher than the +x ring.

## What changed

* `fhand.py` generator (parameters in `export/hands_params_final.json`): 11 palm rings with C1 weld to the ring (forearm taper continued, blend to a flattened palm), lobed knuckle ring, 4 slit fingers (PCHIP width / depth taper, PIP / DIP dorsal bumps, palmar pads, bent axis, slight splay, round 6-row caps), thumb from a root hole on the palmar-radial corner with a short outward root lift, Catmull-Rom axis with MCP / IP nodes, IP knuckle, thenar mound and a smooth-union web fillet. Thumb ring angles are resampled uniformly (the loop-derived angles gave tiny pole faces).
* Placement: cubic Hermite forward shear (start slope = the forearm lean 0.157, 30.9 mm over 109 mm, max local palm-axis tilt ~21 deg) so the weld is C1 and the hand lands on the plate.
* Pose / shape fit (`fit_f.py`, ~12 bounded parameters, random search, 0.2-0.5 s per evaluation): cost = silhouette XOR vs the plate masks + profile landmarks (finger tips, hand band) + geometric sanity (thumb hidden in profile, thumb clearance >= 2.5 mm, web dihedral <= 70, wrist slope). Palm turn pinned to 0 (silhouettes cannot constrain it; the fit flipped between -10 and +20 at equal cost, the profile plate says ~0) and the hand direction to the forearm's 3.8 deg outward lean.
* Writer: the old 2,700 hand triangles were removed, their 1,326 vertices reused as slots, 828 appended; custom normals handled as in the limbs chat.
