## What each clip does (seconds)
* **Emote_Scuba** - 0.00-0.30 the left hand rises, its pinch pad is put on the nose tip (contact-solved every frame, 1 mm), the right (racket) hand lifts the racket beside the face; 0.30-1.50 the racket waves from the wrist (2.4 Hz) while the knees open and close twice (period 0.6 s); 1.50-1.68 hold; back to READY by 2.0.
* **Emote_Thrust** - 0.25-1.25 two hip thrusts (pelvis forward 0.19 m, pelvis pitch 17 -> -13 deg, trunk leans back, knees straighten), both fists up (left fist closed, right fist on the racket), fists pump forward with each thrust, a hold to 1.40, settle to READY. The run's pelvis moves 0.05 m; "same size as the run" was read as at-least-run-size hips with the run's arm posture.
* **Emote_Spike** - an aggressive one-arm slam: racket cocked high behind the head with the back arched and the free arm flung out (0.28), a 0.06 s whip down (about 10 m/s at the wrist) with the trunk folding to 48 deg and a left-foot stamp, the racket leaves the hand at 0.40, hits the court head first at 0.47 (one contact), bounces to 0.98 m, flies back and snaps into the right hand at 1.033 s; both hands go to the hips (elbows out), shoulders heave, a hard head shake, READY at 2.0. Faces are painted: the anger is body language.
* **Intro_Wave** - the left hand rises beside the head, palm forward, and waves twice from the wrist (+-40 deg); the racket hand is on its side pose from 0.20 s and does not move until 1.70; READY at 2.0.
* **Intro_BringIt** - the racket comes out and points at the other player (within 3 deg of forward, within 7 deg of horizontal) and stays; the free hand beckons: palm up toward the body, fingers tuck in one after the other, three times; the left foot steps (0.85-1.35) and the right foot steps (1.55-2.05) in place; READY at 3.0.
* **Intro_Pushups** - squat and put the racket down on the court at the right (leaves the hand at 0.433 s, lies from 0.57 s), hands walk out to a toe-planted plank, two push-ups (0.46 s per rep), hands walk back, the racket is picked up (back in the hand at 2.433 s), stand, READY. The feet never move (toes planted; the Root and the feet are where READY has them), the body extends 1.4 m forward of the feet.

## Findings worth keeping
* **The racket is drawn at 1.0, not 1.3, on the match heroes in the real game**: every clip FBX carries constant scale curves on the racket nodes (`removeConstantScaleCurves: 0` in all metas) and the animator resets the driver's 1.3 on the first evaluation (trace `racketScale` = 1.0000 in ReadyIdle and in every emote). The two floor contacts (the Spike bounce, the lying racket) are therefore authored for 1.0 (`RK_AUTHOR`); the pre-registered Blender checks still measure at 1.3 and pass. If the scale override is ever fixed, the Spike racket will sink up to 13 cm into the court at the slam.
* Every FBX in this pipeline carries baked curves on the bone-parented racket nodes with 0.05-0.14 deg of float noise (shipped Forehand / ReadyIdle too): an exact "racket == socket" test on re-imported FBX data is impossible; in the game the distance is 0.0000 m.
* A racket that is world-fixed while the hand moves fast is drawn from 30 fps local curves: the interpolation wobbles it by up to 2 cm. Put-down / pick-up are therefore done in whole-frame height steps (a snap into the hand), never a slow hover.
* READY must be the ReadyIdle CLIP's frame 0 (its tracked left-elbow swivel differs from the engine's default READY by 14 deg on the male).
* Bone-parented children are baked by the FBX exporter whatever the options (`bake_anim_use_all_bones` makes no difference).
* Chest-frame hand targets stop making sense once the trunk is folded (the Spike, the Pushups): those clips are keyed in the world frame. Hand paths that cut through the shoulder flip the arm; every such path has a routing key.
* `TennisMatch` is a struct: `game.Match` is a copy; the film bumps the real score through the boxed private field.
