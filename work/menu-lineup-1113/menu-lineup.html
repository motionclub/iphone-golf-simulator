The lighting

v1 lit the heroes like a photo studio. It was bright, but pale and cut off from the venue. v2 goes back to today's approach: the hero gets the venue's own sun and sky. Three things change so the whole character reads:

What v2 does

LightSettingWhy
SunThe venue's own direction and colour, at the same strength in every venueHeroes match the backdrop, with real shadows on the ground. A very low sun is raised to 28° so it doesn't leave half the face black.
Sky + groundThe venue's sky colour from above, its ground colour bounced from belowCool tops, warm undersides: what sells "outdoors"
Front fillSoft, from the camera, never below a set floorThe shadow side and legs never go black, even in the dark alley
Back lightFaint, sky colourSeparates the heroes from the blurred backdrop
GradeWarm, a little more colour, soft highlight roll-offKeeps the old sunny feel without orange skin or blown whites

Readability check (front hero)

  • Darkest 5% of the body: luma 51–54 in tennis and bowling, 30 in golf (navy trousers)
  • Share of the body near black: 1.2% in tennis and bowling
  • Whites (socks, shoes, skirts) stay white, with their folds still visible.

Found on the way

  • The golf hero export ships a much paler default skin (0.95 / 0.82 / 0.69) than tennis and bowling (0.74 / 0.57 / 0.45). In the golf mockup the front hero uses the tennis tone so the lighting compares fairly. In the game the golf hero would look paler than in tennis.

What it takes in the game

Formation

  • Four fixed slots on the menu stage, filled in join order. Party heroes already arrive with the party.
  • Each slot plays its idle at a different offset; a new arrival waves.
  • Name plates follow each head in screen space; empty slots show the invite marker.

Lighting

  • The menu already applies each venue's sun and sky. Add a front fill with a minimum strength, a faint back light, and a fixed sun strength across venues.
  • Replace the plaza grade on the menu with a gentler warm grade.
  • The existing shadow catcher, plus a soft contact shadow per slot.

Phone cost

  • Four full-detail heroes is roughly 900k triangles, so the back row should use the lower-detail hero.
  • Measure memory on a phone with a full party before shipping.

Method: heroes drawn with the app's SceneKit copy of the Unity heroes (same meshes, colours and clips; ported shaders), lit from each venue's saved light settings, then composited on the shipped venue backdrops with rendered floor shadows. Face numbers are mean brightness (0–255) and red minus blue over the same face area. A Unity capture is still needed before this counts as done.
