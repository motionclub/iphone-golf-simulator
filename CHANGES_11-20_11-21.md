## Tennis

### Setup: point, hold, calibrate, timing check (local-setup-1120)
- One simple flow, solo or with friends: every phone points at the TV and holds the HOLD ring. The TV flashes. Then each phone gets the timing check (Start / Skip), the drill, and Ready.
- New TV screens from Adnan's `ArtDir/ui/tv-results-calibration` designs, drawn by the app over the game (`TennisCalibrationTV.swift`, `SportsDisplays.showTennisSetupOnTV`):
  - the scan banner and reticle;
  - "Get ready for flashes";
  - "TV latency NNN ms";
  - the drill, with the ball dropping onto the line and the swing pips;
  - "Ready to play".
- Two phones on one TV:
  - the TV flashes only after everyone has pointed;
  - the second phone gets its timing check once the TV phone has measured the TV;
  - both drills start together, on the shared network clock, 2.5 s after the last Start;
  - a chip per player shows where each one is.
- Unity's old timing panel is hidden (`TennisHud.NativeTimingCheck`). Unity sends `timingStart` / `timingSwing` so the TV can draw the drill.
- The "still setting up" notice moved from 60 s to 120 s.

### Quick tennis start (onboarding-1121)
- **A TV it already knows:** point → play. No flashes, no drill, no Ready tap: about 12–15 s plus loading, against 45–75 s before.
  - The TV's result is saved under a stronger key: audio route type, uid, name and screen size (`sports.tv.picture.v3` / `sports.tv.chain.v3`).
- **A new TV:** shorter countdowns (3 s), and the drill ends early once 5 swings agree, so about 30 s.
- The lag learned during each match is saved at match end, so the next match starts better calibrated. "Recheck timing" is still in the menus.
- You can point at the TV while the court loads.
- The TV direction is kept for 10 minutes after a match (`motion.idle()` instead of `motion.stop()`), so a rematch skips setup.
- **Fix:** in two-phone play the host dropped the hold / pointed / go / check setup messages (`setupStep` filter in MultiplayerService).

## Phone casting: one phone is the screen (phone-casting-1120)
- Golf and bowling only. Tennis needs a big screen.
- New menu rows: "Phone screen" (`bay-golf-cast`, `bay-bowling-cast`). This phone shows the game sideways like a TV, and each friend's phone nearby joins and plays. It runs on the existing party transport: nearby when phones are close, Game Center otherwise, including mobile data.
- Lobby rules:
  - the screen is never seated (`castScreen`, `minimumCompetitors`);
  - the Bonjour TXT carries `screen=1`;
  - players get the banner "Play golf on X's screen?".
- Golf: 1 golfer is allowed. The screen waits for each new hole. Players get snapshots at 6 Hz plus one on each change, about 0.6–0.7 MB/min instead of 3–4.
- Bowling: the bowler's phone streams the setup and sends the throw at release, then the pinfall. The screen mirrors the setup and never releases the ball itself.
- Guests can ride on a player's phone ("+ Guest"). Players' phones show a "Waiting for the screen phone" banner while the screen is away. The party switch "This phone is the screen" is there too.

## Added later on 11-21 (after the first combine)

### TV and mirroring (bowling-freeze / AirPlay chat, branch `fix/bowl-freeze-airplay-1121`)
- **Screen Mirroring on iOS 27 shows the game, not the controller.** On iOS 27, mirroring only shows an app's own TV picture if the app registers for it. The code that does that sat behind a check (`canImport(UIKit, _version: 9500)`) that no SDK passes. So every build left it out, 0.1.6 (25) included, and iOS 27 phones mirrored the controller. Now:
  - the check is `9127`, the iOS 27 SDK's UIKit version;
  - the registration lives on the phone window's root and moves if that root changes;
  - it is switched on explicitly and logged (`external display accessory registered`).
- **Connect TV sheet** (`ConnectTV.swift`): one sheet everywhere the game needs a TV.
  - It loops a drawn demo of the two Control Center taps and shows "Looking for your TV…". Once the TV is there it shows "Connected to <TV>" and carries on by itself.
  - Golf and bowling also offer "Play on this phone".
  - It opens from: a gold "Connect TV" chip on the no-TV menu; starting tennis or the tennis lesson with no TV; the pause menu when the TV drops; and the tennis lesson's safety card.
  - **Fix:** starting tennis with no TV went to the TV-only `.connect` screen, so on the phone nothing showed and the menu closed.
- **Bowling freeze (the bowler stands still, holding the ball does nothing, mostly on first launch):**
  - Unity paused any solo game after 0.5 s with no motion samples, and nothing showed that pause in bowling. A slow sensor start on a cold launch tripped it.
  - Bowling is now exempt from that check (`NativeSportsSession.Update`), and the first sample after a resume gets 2 s in every sport.
  - Any bowling pause now opens the pause menu with Resume.
