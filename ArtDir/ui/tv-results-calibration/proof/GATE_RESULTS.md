# TV design review

- GATE: RESULTS PASS. The TV preview shows everyone who played, a winner, placement, and sport-specific scoring. Tennis labels Win and Loss. Golf's scorecard totals match the results.
- GATE: XP PASS. Continue opens a separate payout. Changing the selected player updates the amount and level progress. The golf example adds to 225 XP and leaves 25 of 266 XP at Level 2.
- GATE: CALIBRATION PASS. Chromium verified that the intro waits for Start, the scan screen waits for scan confirmation, and the latency screen waits for Start calibration. Switching sports during the warning canceled the flash sequence. The recording shows the warning for exactly 36 frames at 24 fps, followed by four full-screen flashes. The swing drill counts down from five and records seven simulated swings after two warm-up bounces. The 37-second walkthrough and eight stage captures are 1280 × 720. [Recording](calibration/tennis-calibration-walkthrough.mp4) · [Warning](calibration/03-warning.jpg) · [Latency](calibration/05-latency.jpg) · [Ball drop](calibration/07-ball-drop.jpg)
- GATE: TV LAYOUT PASS. Chromium loaded the bundled Kanit font and scene art. All 12 captures keep the panels and controls inside the 16:9 frame.
- GATE: MAP COLORS PASS. Cliffside, Tropical Resort, and Club Lanes each use their game skin colors for the panel, winner row, buttons, and XP meter. Chromium verified at least 4.5:1 contrast for the sampled text and 3:1 for the meter fill against its track in all three palettes. The Tropical Resort palette also carries through the calibration screens.
- GATE: VARIATIONS PASS. Broadcast uses horizontal tiles, Podium has a separate first-place stand, and Scorebook uses a centered ruled sheet. Chromium captured 15 frames per design at 1280 × 720 with the panels and buttons inside the TV frame. Design switching keeps the current stage and selected XP player. Scorecard, standings, XP selection, replay actions, and countdown controls passed in all three designs. [Comparison](tv-variation-comparison.jpg)
- GATE: TENNIS AND BOWLING SCOREBOOKS PASS. Box Score, Open Book, and Score Slip render results, XP, and replay at 1280 × 720. Chromium checked table cell fit, frame totals, win and loss labels, reward selection, and the replay controls. Selecting Golf from these designs returns to the chosen Scorebook. [Results comparison](tennis-bowling-results-variations.jpg) · [XP comparison](tennis-bowling-xp-variations.jpg)
- GATE: POST-GAME MOTION PASS. Snap In, Page Turn, and Spotlight each show victory, standings, XP, and replay for Golf, Tennis, and Bowling. The winner's score matches the standings and the final XP matches the sample award. Chromium verified live play, stop, manual continuation, and player selection. The three side-by-side recordings are 1920 × 432 at 24 fps and approximately 10.2 seconds each. [Snap In](postgame/snap-all-sports.mp4) · [Page Turn](postgame/turn-all-sports.mp4) · [Spotlight](postgame/spotlight-all-sports.mp4)
- GATE: WINNER REVEALS PASS. Score Strip, Venue Plaque, and Victory Type show the sample winner and correct score across nine 1280 × 720 captures. Chromium checked plaque text fit, TV bounds, clearance from the Results button, and the Snap In sequence through Results, XP, and Replay for every combination. The finished tennis recording shows each reveal reaching the same Box Score. [Three-sport comparison](victory/winner-reveal-comparison.jpg) · [Tennis animation](victory/tennis-winner-comparison.mp4)
- GATE: FINAL VENUE PLAQUE PASS. The TV preview defaults to Venue Plaque with Snap In. Chromium verified that the name and uppercase WINS share one line, with the 33–39 px score on its own line at 1280 × 720. Both lines fit all three signs and clear the Results button. Sport switching and the full sequence passed. [Final plaques](victory/final/venue-plaque-all-sports.jpg)

These gates cover the design preview and its sample data. The scene backgrounds come from clean Unity captures for tennis and golf, and the existing Club Lanes map image for bowling.
