# Motion Sports TV results and calibration

Open [the design preview](index.html). Golf opens in Scorebook. Tennis and Bowling open in Box Score. Select a sport above the TV frame. The screen controls below it jump to a specific state.

## Winner reveal

The preview opens on **Venue Plaque**. The winner's name and **WINS** share a line, with the final score in large type underneath. Select **Play sequence** to replay Snap In. The selected winner layout follows you when you switch sports. [Final Venue Plaque across all three sports](proof/victory/final/venue-plaque-all-sports.jpg)

The first review compared three directions. [Three-sport comparison](proof/victory/winner-reveal-comparison.jpg) · [Tennis animation comparison](proof/victory/tennis-winner-comparison.mp4)

- **Score Strip** puts the winner and final score in a compact lower-left scoreboard.
- **Venue Plaque** sets the winner into each map's existing sculpted sign.
- **Victory Type** puts the winner in oversized Kanit over the game view with the final score beneath.

## Post-game motion

Select **Play sequence** to run Snap In from the winner reveal through the standings, XP count-up, and replay options. The winner stays highlighted at the top of the standings. Continue steps through the screens manually. The original recordings show the earlier winner treatment.

- **Snap In** lands the winner plaque, then slides in the standings and their rows. [All three sports](proof/postgame/snap-all-sports.mp4)
- **Page Turn** pivots each panel along the edge of the Scorebook, with a first-place stamp on the opening page. [All three sports](proof/postgame/turn-all-sports.mp4)
- **Spotlight** dims the venue around the winner, then uses slower fades for the standings and XP. [All three sports](proof/postgame/spotlight-all-sports.mp4)

The examples are approximately ten seconds with sample results. The browser preview uses captured game scenes and supports reduced motion.

## Results flow

1. Keep the last game view on-screen. Show the winner and rank everyone who played. Tennis shows games and Win/Loss. Golf shows to-par and strokes, with an optional hole-by-hole scorecard. Bowling shows score and strikes.
2. Switch to XP on Continue. Show the award, its sources, level progress, and Level up when earned. Use the player tabs to review the other players' awards. The preview calculates each amount and level from sample data using the app's level curve.
3. Keep the final menu to the replay action, venue change, and Main Menu. The preview shows which action was selected.

For a solo golf round, the card should lead with the score against par. A tied score should share the placement unless the sport has a tiebreak in effect. Use the game mode's actual result data for each row.

## Calibration

Select **Calibration** above the TV. The intro explains the scan and swing timing, then waits for **Start**. The scan screen shows one instruction and a reticle. Select **Simulate phone scan** below the preview to confirm the scan.

The TV shows **Get ready for flashes** for 1.5 seconds, then runs four flashes. The next screen shows one latency reading and waits for **Start calibration**. The swing drill uses a five-second countdown, two warm-up bounces, and seven progress pips. The preview simulates the scan, the 128 ms reading, and the swings.

## Type and material

Use the menu's bundled Kanit. ExtraBold Italic for headings and primary actions, ExtraBold for scores, and Medium for body copy. Cliffside pairs limestone and sea blue. Tropical Resort uses sand, honey wood, and lagoon teal. Club Lanes uses navy and cream with walnut trim and brass highlights. The colors come from the game's map skins and carry through the winner row, actions, and progression. Tennis calibration uses the Tropical Resort palette. The TV frames are design composites at 1280 × 720.
