# Brand sculpted UI — mockups first

The pasted brief is copied in BRIEF.md. Produce 15 square 1254×1254 concept boards for review using built-in imagegen. No implementation, game edits or commits. Use one palette across maps; map imagery and sculpted title miniatures alone may retain their native material colors. Cream is normalized to #FFF4DF from the prior approved-style preview; other tokens come from MSBrand.swift/BrandColors.cs.

Proof lives under proof/. Check every board for requested content, shared palette, readable data, username-only labels, no sport-category labels and four-notch power meters. Regenerate any failed board and record why. Phone-size readability is assessed for the individual depicted phone screen at 402pt equivalent, not a 15-board overview reduced to phone width. Review-caption/hex legends are documentation rather than in-product data.
