Built-in imagegen. Approved direction: references/approved-play-menu.png. Do not change the approved main menu; create other menus. General in-game UI redesign is excluded. Separately requested Daily Hole in One and in-game onboarding mockups are tracked outside this menu set.

## 01-map-chooser

Use case: ui-mockup. ONE EXACT 1254x1254 square PNG MENU-ONLY design board.
Reference 1 is the USER-APPROVED sleek Play menu style: match its quiet layout, restrained spacing, modest wordmark, flat type, matte navy scrim, cream selection row and small gold edge. Do not remake that main menu. Design the OTHER MENU specified below in the same family.
LATEST SCOPE: menus only. Absolutely NO in-game HUD, power bar, 72%, stamina, scoreboard overlay, serve meter, TOSS, swing control, radar, action control deck, gameplay calibration, pause overlay or results panel. The POWER sample accidentally present in reference 1 is NOT part of the approved menu content and MUST NOT be copied.
Typography: clean moderately weighted rounded sans serif, flat and carefully kerned, no puffy/embossed/outlined text. Brand only: one small MotionSports wordmark per screen in white Motion/gold Sports italic. Large open scenery/hero area, clean baselines, generous negative space. Calm brand colors #061A4B/#0B2878/#003EB8, gold #F5C542 for one primary action, cream #FFF4DF for secondary actions/selection. Glow used only as a very fine focus accent, never bloom. Shallow 1–2px button lip at most. No thick borders, plastic frames, nested capsules, sparkles, swooshes, decorative rays or glossy bevels. No map-material UI skins. Native map art/item thumbnails/hero outfits can have their actual colors.
Usernames only ADNAN, MILO, JUNE: no YOU/host/player1/rival role badges. No sport-category words TENNIS/GOLF/BOWLING anywhere. No currencies, battlepass, made-up features or unrelated live game data.
Square presentation has small plain CONCEPT and one short menu title outside; NO design-review slogans, explanatory paragraphs, footer bullets or claims like build-ready. One large landscape menu screen takes most of the board; secondary small MENU state only when specifically requested. No device hardware. All controls safe-inset and legible, hierarchy main title→choices→primary action. References beyond 1 provide specific scene/content only, not styling.
MENU REQUIREMENTS:
One large landscape Choose a map screen. A disciplined 5x2 grid of ten well-modeled miniature scene objects with flat names: TROPICAL RESORT, SKY TOWER, MAGMA CRATER, CLIFFSIDE, WILD ISLES; MAGMA OPEN, CLUB LANES, NEON NIGHTS, TIKI SHORES, MAGMA LANES. Each miniature sits directly on a calm navy/blue field with a small cream name area, no heavy card rim. SKY TOWER selected by a slim gold keyline, small understated check and cream label. Native colors stay within miniatures only. Modest top-left MotionSports, ADNAN profile top-right, quiet header Choose a map. Gold Play bottom-right and cream Back bottom-left. No loading strip, percentages or player-readiness list on this board. All ten map names appear once. Footer outside screen blank apart from a tiny caption.
