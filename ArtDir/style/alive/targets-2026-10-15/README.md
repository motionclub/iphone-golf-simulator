# Female hero — expressive character direction
2026-10-15. **Concept review only. Implementation awaits Adnan's instruction.**

The previous pass was too small. These targets propose a coordinated overhaul of acting, garment form, and material readability. The personality is **a playful competitor**: engaged and determined in play, delighted by a good point, briefly frustrated by a miss, generous toward an opponent, ready for another round.

## The three boards
1. [The playful competitor](01-playful-competitor.png): overall appeal, asymmetric posture, expressive face, clear cloth silhouette.
2. [She has something to say](02-acting-range.png): six different intentions expressed through the whole body and face. These are key-pose concepts, not animation clips or proof of transitions.
3. [Clothes with weight](03-clothes-with-weight.png): tennis and golf construction; collar stands, cuff air gaps, thick turned edges, fabric ease and folds responding to the pose.

Board 03 governs garment fit: overlap the waistband and keep the top relaxed. The small exposed waist on board 01 is not a requirement. Existing colors remain customizable. Texture detail in close-ups is subordinate to broad garment shapes.

## What makes this a meaningful step
- Replace interchangeable neutral behavior with authored attitude and context: readiness, concentration, release, disappointment and recovery.
- Pose face, gaze, shoulders, torso, hips and hands as one intention. Merely adding a smile or a breathing oscillation cannot meet this target.
- Give garments their own silhouette and volume. A collar must stand away, a cuff must enclose space, and a hem must have thickness. Do not rely on painted creases.
- Establish clear material hierarchy: soft skin, matte knit, structured woven skirt, rubber shoes, restrained eye highlights.

The reference subject remains the female hero. Preserve her actual body/face identity and proportions when implementing. Generated concepts approximate identity; they are not replacement modeling plates. Do not enlarge her eyes or shrink her face/body to reproduce incidental generation drift. No male asset work is authorized by these boards. No game camera, map or lighting changes are proposed.

## Research and interpretation
- **Wii Sports, 2006:** Nintendo's developers discuss how simple characters helped players identify themselves in the activity. Our takeaway: readable action and player connection matter more than surface detail. [Iwata Asks — Did Anyone Say It Should Look Realistic?](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wii/wii_sports/0/1/)
- **Splatoon, 2015:** Nintendo describes starting from gameplay prototypes and developing the character around the game's function. Our takeaway: identity should be tied to what the character does. [Iwata Asks — Splatoon](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wiiu/splatoon/0/0/)
- **Nintendo Switch Sports, 2022:** the developers say Wii Sports had about 30 character motions and Sportsmates required over 650, including direction- and angle-specific motions for connected limbs. A simple appearance does not imply a small animation effort. Our target needs contextual, coherent acting rather than generic additive motion. [Ask the Developer, chapter 3](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2022/May/Ask-the-Developer-Vol-5-Nintendo-Switch-Sports-2215553.html)
- **Fortnite:** Epic's art-production Q&A points artists to its original art-direction talk and official artist work. The desired transfer here is our art-direction interpretation: confident broad forms, readable materials, and a character who already has attitude in an ordinary outfit. We are not copying skins or claiming a specific Epic recipe. [Epic art-production Q&A](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/ask-epic-art-production-january-25-2024-10-am-et/1626687?page=2) · [Developing the Art of Fortnite](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=498KToofNf8) (linked reference; video contents were not available to inspect through the web reader).

## Proposed future review gates — not run
- With unchanged gameplay camera/light, compare matched before/after rally, idle, golf and outcome sequences. Improvements must read without zooming to a facial crop.
- At roughly 240px character height, focus, victory and disappointment should remain distinguishable without captions; neutral and recovery should not look frozen.
- In neutral front/side views the top and polo must visibly have their own volume. Verify cuff/collar/hem separation and no body clipping in motion.
- Keep body/head source identity and outfit categories; check palette controls with light, dark and saturated colors.
- Show anticipation, contact and settle in muted clips. A still cannot prove weight, timing, foot contact or secondary motion.
- Check real device performance before claiming a runtime pass.
