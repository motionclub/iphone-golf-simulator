## 7. Materials and clothing: specific targets
Skin: warm and clean, softly matte/satin, readable cheek/nose/lip/ear planes, gentle local warmth, no dirty AO stains, flat grey cast, excessive orange/yellow, oily shine or pore noise. Keep sufficient separation of skin from fabric without changing identity. Eye focus and eyelid shapes matter more than giant catchlights.
Tennis top: lime technical-knit V-neck, thick bound neckline/armholes, visible athletic ease around lower ribs/waist, broad meaningful gravity folds, separate hem overlapping the waistband. No abdomen-contour shell.
Golf polo: coral matte knit, collar with stand and underside, layered two-button placket, rounded sleeves with cuff thickness and air gaps, loose torso ease, plain back and turned hem over waistband.
Skorts: ivory tennis/navy golf, broad grouped pleats with depth and real returns, substantial waistband/hem, opaque fitted liner shorts, independent cloth silhouette. No dense radial ribs, rigid cone or paper-thin sheet.
Footwear/cap: preserve recognizable simple shapes, distinct rubber/fabric response, substantial soles and brim, restrained seams. Keep cap separate. Golf glove can remain a separate accessory; absent in neutral masters to expose both hands.
Keep garment base color customizable. Geometry, neutral detail, roughness/normal response and deformation should carry the improvement in light, dark and saturated colors.
Approximate body triangle budget remains 35-50k unless otherwise approved; profile garment/face additions separately. Do not trade mobile readability for invisible texture detail. Avoid crunch compression that damages the face.

## 8. Acting: how neutral becomes soulful
Once authorized, use the expressive sheet as performance intent:
- Readiness: engaged eyes, quiet anticipation, athletic weight.
- Focus: eyes track the play; brow/lids and torso work together.
- Success: a clear release, weight transfer, authentic delighted face, followed by settle.
- Miss: readable but brief disappointment; recover with personality.
- Opponent's good shot: generous social acknowledgment.
- Idle/invitation: a character waiting and thinking, not random perpetual swaying.
Animate eyes/head/torso with purposeful lead and follow, strong readable key poses, anticipation, contact, recovery, secondary motion and controlled holds. Avoid perpetual maximum expression or constant jitter. This pack is not evidence that any of those timings already work.
