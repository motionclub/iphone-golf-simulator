# Tennis hero comparison sheets

**GATE: SIDE-BY-SIDE PASS.** The overview pairs the male and female studies above the corresponding Unity front, right-profile and back views. Figure height is normalized for visual comparison.

**GATE: ANGLE COVERAGE PASS.** Both heroes have four additional front and rear diagonal study views paired with the corresponding Unity headings. The Unity contact sheet contains ten distinct views per hero, including elevated and low shots.

The Unity images are from the November 12 installed-project studio capture of `MatchHero_Male` and `MatchHero_Female`. The studies are conceptual ImageGen views. Their camera angles and anatomy can vary slightly across panels.
