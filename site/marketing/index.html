MotionSports

Party Games for iPhone. Swing your phone, and play golf, tennis and bowling on the big screen.

Your phone is the controller

Mirror your iPhone to an AirPlay-compatible TV, an Apple TV or a Mac, then swing. The phone reads your motion and the game plays out on the TV. Golf and bowling also play on the phone screen alone.

Golf

Take it back and swing through. Read the wind, shape your shots, and play island courses with friends taking turns.

Tennis

Your player runs to the ball; you time the swing and pick the direction. Serve, rally, and charge up a supercharged shot.

Bowling

Hold, step and release. Curve the ball into the pocket, and pass the phone between frames.

Made for parties

  • Play together in the same room by passing the phone, or with each friend on their own phone over your Wi-Fi.
  • Play online with Game Center: invite friends, share a party code, or use Quick Match. Guests can play without signing in.
  • Customise your character in the locker, and celebrate with emotes.
  • Short lessons when you start show you how to swing in each sport.
  • Record the TV picture and share your favourite moments.
