Set up your TV (AirPlay)

Your iPhone is the controller, and the game shows on an AirPlay-compatible TV, an Apple TV or a Mac.

  1. Put your iPhone and your TV (or Apple TV or Mac) on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the iPhone, open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner).
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring and choose your TV, Apple TV or Mac.
  4. Open MotionSports. The game appears on the TV, and your phone shows the controller.
  5. Using a Mac? First turn on System Settings › General › AirDrop & Handoff › AirPlay Receiver.

Golf and bowling can also be played on the phone screen alone, without a TV.

How to play

Clear some space around you, keep a firm grip on the phone, and stand about 2 m back from the screen.

Golf

  • Line up with the aim pad and pick a club. Check the wind arrow first.
  • Tap the swing button, then swing the phone like a club. How far back you take it sets the distance. A smooth, committed swing beats a hard one.
  • Twisting the phone open or closed curves the ball. Keep the face square to hit it straight.
  • Putts are small strokes. In party golf, whoever is farthest from the hole plays next.

Tennis

  • Hold the phone like a racket handle. Point the back of the phone at the TV once to scan the court. This uses the rear camera.
  • Your player runs to the ball for you. Swing as the ball rises to the top of its bounce. Timing matters more than strength.
  • Finish your swing toward the left, middle or right of the screen to aim.
  • To serve, tap TOSS as the meter crosses the middle, then swing as the ball peaks.

Bowling

  • Press and hold the ball on your phone, then swing with your bowler’s steps: push away, back and through.
  • Let go as your arm swings through the bottom. The phone buzzes to tell you when.
  • Turn your wrist as you let go to curve the ball. Taking turns? Pass the phone between frames.

Playing with friends

  • Same room: friends on the same Wi-Fi can join your nearby party, or you can pass one phone around.
  • Online: sign in to Game Center to invite friends, join by party code, or use Quick Match. You can play as a guest without signing in.
  • Chat: quick phrases are always available. Typing is for ages 18 and up. Use Report, Mute or Block from the party menu if someone is bothering you.

Common fixes

No TV found

  • Check that the phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that the TV supports AirPlay.
  • Start Screen Mirroring from Control Center before opening the game, or restart the TV and try again.
  • For a Mac, make sure AirPlay Receiver is on and the Mac is awake.

Lag or delay

  • Move closer to your Wi-Fi router, and pause large downloads or streams on the same network.
  • Restart the TV or Apple TV and the iPhone.
  • In tennis, if you keep swinging late, keep playing: the game adjusts to your display’s delay after a few swings.

Camera access denied (tennis)

  • Tennis movement uses the rear camera. Go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera and turn MotionSports on.
  • Camera images are processed on your phone and not recorded or uploaded. Golf and bowling do not use the camera.

Court scan or calibration problems

  • Stand about 2.5 m from the screen with the rear camera pointing at it while it flashes.
  • Keep the lens clear and the room lit. Blank walls or a covered lens can stop tracking.
  • If the swing feels off, re-centre by pointing the back of the phone at the TV again.

Friends can’t join

  • For nearby play, allow Local Network for MotionSports in iOS Settings, and stay on the same Wi-Fi.
  • For online play, sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings › Game Center) and check your internet connection.

Send us diagnostics

If something keeps going wrong, open the game’s Settings and tap Send diagnostics and send the log to us, for example by email. The log contains game events only, not your identity or camera images.
