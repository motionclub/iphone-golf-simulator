Privacy Policy

MotionSports: Party Games · Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

  • We do not run a game server of our own. Online play goes through Apple’s Game Center, and online status, party codes and invite notifications use Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit).
  • Motion and camera data are processed on your phone. Camera images are not recorded or uploaded. The app has no chat or voice chat, so players cannot message each other.
  • The app does not read your contacts or phone numbers. Text invites open Apple’s Messages sheet, where you pick who gets them.
  • No ads, no advertising identifier, and we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context advertising.

Inviting friends by text message

When you choose to invite someone by text, the app writes a short message with your display name, a party code and a join link, and opens Apple’s Messages compose sheet (or the iOS share sheet). You type or pick the phone number inside Apple’s sheet. The app does not see, read, upload or hash that number or your contacts. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send yourself. Anyone with your party code can ask to join your party, and you choose whether to let them in.

No chat

MotionSports has no typed chat, voice chat or private messaging. Other players see only your Game Center display name, character look, scores and emotes. Quick Match can pair you with Game Center players you do not know, but you cannot exchange messages with them in the app.

What leaves your device, and who receives it

  • Apple Game Center handles sign-in, friends, invitations, matchmaking and the live match data between phones. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) stores the online status record, party codes and invite or join requests described above, in this app’s public database. Records are written under your iCloud account. They are readable by other players of the app who look you up by player ID or party code, and by us. Apple also delivers invite notifications (Apple Push Notification service) if you allow notifications.
  • Apple AirPlay sends the game picture and sound to the TV or Mac you choose. That stays between your devices.
  • Players on your Wi-Fi. Nearby parties connect phones directly over your local network. They share your display name, character look, and game actions with the phones in your lobby only.
  • Other players see your display name, character look, scores and emotes. Pick a display name that does not identify you.
  • Unity Technologies. The game is built with the Unity engine, which may send anonymous technical statistics about your device hardware to Unity. We do not use Unity analytics or advertising services.
  • GitHub hosts these web pages and may log visitors’ IP addresses.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players’ safety.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The app contains no advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep information

  • On your phone: progress, settings and recordings stay until you delete them or the app. The diagnostics log is capped at about 1 MB, with the oldest entries dropped first.
  • Invite and join requests in iCloud: deleted once used, or when they expire (typically within minutes).
  • Online status record and party code in iCloud: kept with no automatic expiry, until you ask us to delete them. The status record is updated while the game is open.
  • Support messages you send us: kept as long as needed to answer them.

How to delete your data

  1. Data on your phone: delete the app (press and hold the icon › Remove App › Delete App). This removes progress, settings, logs and recordings that you have not saved elsewhere.
  2. Data in iCloud (online status and party code): email adnanyonathanbiz@gmail.com with the subject “MotionSports data deletion” and your Game Center display name. We aim to delete it within 30 days and will confirm by email. We may ask a question to check the request is really yours.
  3. Game Center and iCloud accounts belong to Apple. You can manage or delete them in iOS Settings or at privacy.apple.com.

The app does not have a separate account of its own, so there is nothing else to close.

Children’s privacy

MotionSports is rated 12+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no chat or messaging, so players cannot share personal information with each other in the app. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, including California and other US states with privacy laws, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to profile you. You will not be treated differently for asking. To make a request, email adnanyonathanbiz@gmail.com. Someone you authorise may also ask for you. If we turn a request down, you can reply to our answer to ask us to reconsider.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. Data that leaves your phone goes through Apple’s services (Game Center, iCloud, AirPlay), which use encryption in transit. Information stored on your phone is protected by iOS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.
